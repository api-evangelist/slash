Slash is a business banking and finance platform for modern companies — startups, agencies, e-commerce sellers, and enterprises — combining business checking, unlimited virtual and physical corporate cards, ACH/wire and real-time payments, global USD accounts, stablecoin on/off ramps, treasury, invoicing, expense management, and reporting on one platform. Founded in 2021 and valued at $1.4B after a 2026 Series C led by Ribbit Capital, Slash exposes a REST API (currently in Beta) covering accounts, virtual accounts, cards and card groups, transactions, transfers, invoices, contacts and customers, expense reports, webhooks, and FDX-compliant financial data access — plus an official hosted MCP server, Agent Requests with human-in-the-loop approval, and Python and Node.js SDKs for AI-agent and programmatic integration.

**Areas:** Company · Banking · Payments · Financial Services · Fintech · Corporate Cards · Business Banking · Invoicing · Expense Management · Treasury · FDX

## How this profile is made

API Evangelist profiles a company's public API surface the way a developer would find it: the website, developer portal and documentation, the specifications it publishes, its public repositories, and its pricing, status and changelog pages. From that it derives the properties above — contracts split per API, workflows, schemas, collections, governance rules, plans and rate limits — and scores them against the published [Kin Score rubric](https://github.com/api-evangelist/kin-score).

The artifacts are published on [APIs.io](https://apis.io), not in this repository. This repository is the public record for Slash: the place to say something is wrong, missing or out of date, and to ask for a re-score or a removal.
