- [List ACH transfers](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/ach/list.md): Return ACH transfers, including queued transfers awaiting bank details.
- [Retrieve an ACH transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/ach/retrieve.md): Retrieve an ACH transfer. An id belonging to another transfer rail returns the same not-found response as an unknown id.
- [Cancel an ACH transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/ach/cancel.md): Cancel an ACH transfer before it has been submitted to the payment network. A queued ACH can be canceled while awaiting destination details.
- [Create a wire transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/wire/create.md): Send USD to a saved US bank account by domestic wire.
- [List wire transfers](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/wire/list.md): Return domestic wire transfers, newest first.
- [Retrieve a wire transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/wire/retrieve.md): Retrieve a domestic wire transfer. An id belonging to another transfer rail returns the same not-found response as an unknown id.
- [Create an international wire transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/international-wire/create.md): Send USD or a supported foreign currency to an international destination.
- [List international wire transfers](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/international-wire/list.md): Return international wire transfers, newest first.
- [Retrieve an international wire transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/international-wire/retrieve.md): Retrieve an international wire transfer. An id belonging to another transfer rail returns the same not-found response as an unknown id.
- [Create an FX quote](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/international-wire/fx-quotes/create.md): Quote the USD debit for an exact foreign-currency amount.
- [Retrieve an FX quote](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/international-wire/fx-quotes/retrieve.md): Retrieve the current status and pricing of an FX quote.
- [Create a realtime transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/realtime/create.md): Send USD to a saved RTP- or FedNow-capable destination.
- [List realtime transfers](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/realtime/list.md): Return RTP and FedNow transfers, newest first.
- [Retrieve a realtime transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/realtime/retrieve.md): Retrieve an RTP or FedNow transfer. An id belonging to another transfer rail returns the same not-found response as an unknown id.
- [Create a book transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/book/create.md): Move USD between virtual accounts in the same Account.
- [List book transfers](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/book/list.md): Return transfers between Slash balances, newest first.
- [Retrieve a book transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/book/retrieve.md): Retrieve a book transfer. An id belonging to another transfer rail returns the same not-found response as an unknown id.
- [List transfers](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/list.md): Return transfers across every supported payment rail, newest first. Use `type` to select one rail.
- [Retrieve a transfer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/transfers/retrieve.md): Retrieve the latest state of one accessible transfer.
- [List bills](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/list.md): List bills, soonest due first; bills without a due date come first, and bills due the same day are ordered by id.
- [Create a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/create.md): Create a bill as a `draft`. Only the line items are required.
- [Get a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/retrieve.md): Get a bill with its line items, documents and payments.
- [Update a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/update.md): Update a bill. Only the fields you send change. Line items and amounts can only change while the bill is `draft` or `rejected`.
- [Delete a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/delete.md): Delete a bill that hasn't been paid: one that is `draft`, `rejected` or `pending_approval`.
- [Void a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/void.md): Cancel the bill's payments that are awaiting approval or scheduled and return it to `draft`.
- [Link a payment to a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/payments/add.md): Record a transfer or transaction you already made as a payment on the bill.
- [Unlink a payment from a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/payments/remove.md): Remove a payment from a bill without touching the money.
- [Attach documents to a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/documents/add.md): Attach uploaded documents to a bill. Documents already on the bill are ignored.
- [Detach a document from a bill](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/documents/remove.md): Remove a document from a bill. The document itself is kept.
- [Get the bill inbox](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/inbox/get.md): Get the legal entity's accounts payable email address and the senders allowed to email it. Invoices sent there become draft bills.
- [Allowlist an inbox sender](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/inbox/senders/add.md): Allow an email address or a whole domain to send bills to the inbox. Public email domains such as `gmail.com` can't be allowed as a domain.
- [Remove an inbox sender](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/bills/inbox/senders/remove.md): Stop accepting bills from an allowed sender. Bills they already sent are kept.
- [List invoices](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/list.md): List invoices. Add `includeSummary=true` for the count and outstanding balance of every invoice the filters match.
- [Create an invoice](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/create.md): Create an invoice and email it to the customer, or schedule it with `scheduledSendDate`.
- [Get an invoice](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/retrieve.md): Get an invoice.
- [Update an invoice](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/update.md): Edit a `scheduled` or `unpaid` invoice.
- [Send an invoice now](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/send.md): Email a `scheduled` invoice now instead of on its `scheduledSendDate`. See [Schedule or send](/docs/v2/invoices/creating-and-sending#schedule-or-send).
- [Send a payment reminder](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/send-reminder.md): Email the customer a reminder for an `unpaid` or `partially_paid` invoice, at most once a day.
- [Void an invoice](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/void.md): Mark an invoice as `void` so it can no longer be paid.
- [Get an invoice's payment instructions](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/payment-instructions.md): Get the bank and crypto details a customer can pay the invoice to, as shown on its PDF and payment page.
- [Record a payment on an invoice](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/payments/add.md): Record that a sent invoice was paid, by a deposit Slash didn't match on its own or outside Slash.
- [Remove a payment from an invoice](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/payments/remove.md): Remove a payment from an invoice without touching the money.
- [List invoice series](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/series/list.md): List recurring invoice series.
- [Create an invoice series](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/series/create.md): Start invoicing a customer on a schedule. See [Create a series](/docs/v2/invoices/recurring-invoices#create-a-series).
- [Get an invoice series](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/series/retrieve.md): Get a recurring invoice series.

- [Skip the next invoice in a series](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/series/skip.md): Void an `active` series' scheduled invoice and schedule the one after it.
- [Cancel an invoice series](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/series/cancel.md): Stop an `active` series.

- [Get a customer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/customers/retrieve.md): Get a customer.
- [Update a customer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/customers/update.md): Update a customer. Only the fields you send change. Existing invoices keep the name, email and address they were issued with until you edit them.
- [Archive a customer](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/customers/archive.md): Mark a customer as `archived`. Archiving an archived customer returns it unchanged.

- [Create a catalog item](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/catalog-items/create.md): Save a product or service to reuse on invoices. See [Catalog items](/docs/v2/invoices/creating-and-sending#catalog-items).
- [Get a catalog item](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/catalog-items/retrieve.md): Get a catalog item.
- [Update a catalog item](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/catalog-items/update.md): Update a catalog item. Only the fields you send change, and `null` clears `description`. Invoices that already use the item keep their line items.
- [Archive a catalog item](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/catalog-items/archive.md): Mark a catalog item as `archived`. Archiving an archived item returns it unchanged.
- [Unarchive a catalog item](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/invoices/catalog-items/unarchive.md): Mark an archived catalog item as `active` again. Unarchiving an active item returns it unchanged.

- [Delete an accounting mapping](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/accounting/mappings/delete.md): Delete a mapping. Entries it already coded keep their values.
- [Get accounting intelligence settings](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/accounting/intelligence/retrieve.md): Get which dimensions the accounting intelligence agent codes automatically.
- [Update accounting intelligence settings](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/accounting/intelligence/update.md): Turn the accounting intelligence agent on or off, or choose which dimensions it codes. Changes apply to entries coded from now on.

- [Get a document](https://docs.slash.com/api-reference/documents/retrieve.md): Get a document with a fresh `downloadUrl`, valid for about 15 minutes.
